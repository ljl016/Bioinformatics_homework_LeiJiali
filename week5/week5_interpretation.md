# Week 5 Homework 1 Interpretation

We compared treated with control samples using a DESeq2 model that adjusted for batch (`~ batch + condition`). PCA of variance-stabilized counts showed complete separation by condition along PC1, which explained 24.1% of variance. Batch structure was more evident on PC2, but no sample was removed because PCA alone is insufficient evidence for exclusion. Using `padj < 0.05` and `|shrunken log2 fold change| >= 1`, 60 genes were significant: 36 were higher and 24 lower in treated samples. The result supports a treatment-associated transcriptional response, but the generic gene identifiers and absent functional annotation prevent pathway- or mechanism-level claims.
